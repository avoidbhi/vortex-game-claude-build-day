        VORTEX

        A board game that teaches itself, then plays you.

        1. 1 · The board

          Three rings, eight spokes. You are Ember, the solid amber stones. Frost, the ringed blue stones, is the computer.

        2. 2 · Step

          Each turn, step one stone to a neighbouring node: along its ring, or one spoke in or out.

        3. 3 · Push

          Step onto a Frost stone and it is shoved outward. On the rim, or with the node behind it taken, it is captured.

        4. 4 · Win

          Claim the core: get three stones onto the inner ring. Keep them there through Frost’s reply and you win.
